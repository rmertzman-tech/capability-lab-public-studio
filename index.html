Case-First Simulation Studio

Schelling and Coordination Under Heat are active classroom engines. This pass aligns the public case layer with the Chapters 1–5 teaching guide and adds a reusable timeline player for PRF landscapes, Challenger/SAGE, evidence-policy comparison, Schelling prediction, and care-under-pressure demonstrations.

Teaching sequence

Use this Lab by contrast. First show the traditional simplified model. Then ask what the expanded Capability Lab view reveals about hidden variables, agency-supporting conditions, and repair.

Recommended use:

For Schelling, start with the Traditional Demo and then compare it to Emergent Complexity, Stress Test, or Repair-Oriented View. For Coordination, start with the Traditional Coordination Demo, then compare it with the expanded views to ask whether visible cooperation is ethically healthy.

Active engines

  • Schelling / Layered Emergence
  • Coordination Under Heat
  • Warning Under Pressure / Challenger timeline engine

Instructor Toolkit

This build is designed for live teaching. The Coordination output now separates technical cooperation from ethical coordination.

  • Traditional Coordination shows trust, clarity, information, and heat.
  • Expanded Coordination adds voice, fairness, power, credibility, care burden, translation, memory, and repair.
  • Use the dashboard to ask whether cooperation is constructive or merely compliant.
